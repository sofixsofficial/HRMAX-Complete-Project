## 7. Claims to REMOVE From Your Website (these are what sink credibility)

1. **"50+ team members"** — LinkedIn shows 1. Remove, or list only what you can document.
2. **"500+ projects" / "100+ projects, 8+ countries"** — unverifiable. Say "delivered projects across web, mobile, and business software."
3. **ISO 27001 / ISO 9001 / "Great Place To Work"** — no proof found. Remove until actually certified.
4. **AWS / Google / Microsoft / Oracle / Shopify partner logos** — none verifiable in partner directories. Remove.
5. **"98% client satisfaction"** — no review base to back it.
6. **Conflicting founding years** — pick one (2020 per LinkedIn) and be consistent everywhere.
