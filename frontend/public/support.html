Support

This page provides support contact information for the HR MAX mobile app. HR MAX is a workforce management tool for organizations. The app is free to download and does not sell digital content or subscriptions.

Contact support

For help with your account or the app, email info@hrmax.in. You can also contact your organization's HR or master administrator directly.

Accounts are created and managed by your organization's administrator. Individual users cannot self-register in the app.

Forgot your password?

Use Forgot Password on the app's sign-in screen. A one-time password will be sent to the email address registered for your account.

App access

Access is provisioned by your organization's administrator. If you do not have an account or need access changes, ask your administrator or email the support address above.
